# Dash4Earth — Sustainability Intelligence Platform

## Business Scenario

The [Gies College of Business](https://gies.illinois.edu/) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign had a sustainability research dashboard showcasing 426 faculty publications across all 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals — but it had a critical problem: **nobody was actually using it.**

The dashboard showed data. It didn't serve people.

Students couldn't find research papers. Faculty had no way to discover collaborators. Industry partners found academic data with zero business context. And nobody could ask it a question.

For the **CPRBS Case Competition at Gies**, our team was challenged to transform this static archive into a platform people would actually use.

As **Product Manager**, I led a cross-functional team of 5 to redesign the platform, define the product vision, and deliver a working prototype — competing against 20+ teams.

I identified the core user personas, proposed the AI chatbot architecture, and originated the Sustainability Consulting Initiative. Together with my teammates, we rebuilt the platform from the ground up around three distinct user experiences.

---

## The Problem

Three core failures in the original dashboard:

- **Hard to interpret** — Dense SDG visualizations with no links to papers, authors, or faculty
- **No interactivity** — A static tool that could show *what* existed but couldn't answer *who, why, or how*
- **One-size-fits-all** — A student, a professor, and a sustainability director need completely different things. The dashboard treated them identically

---

## Target Personas

I identified the Student and Faculty personas. My teammate Eugenie surfaced the Industry angle — the three together defined our entire product strategy.

**🎓 Student (Sophia)** — Researching SDG topics for coursework. Needed direct links to papers, authors, and faculty — not a generic chart that sent her to Google Scholar.

**👩‍🏫 Faculty (Dr. Chen)** — Searching for collaborators on climate research. Needed to filter colleagues by SDG, department, and publication — not email the department office blindly.

**🏢 Industry (Marcus)** — A sustainability director under ESG reporting pressure. Needed research-backed strategy organized by business challenge — not academic data with no business context.

---

## Our Solution: One Dashboard, Three Tailored Experiences

Instead of one generic view, we designed **three specialized portals** — each built from the ground up for how that user actually thinks.

**Student Portal** — Click any SDG → see linked research papers, authors, departments, publication years, and direct article links. Filter by topic, year, or faculty. Find a research angle in minutes, not hours.

**Faculty Portal** — Research impact metrics (426 publications, 90 active researchers, +12% growth). Filter faculty by SDG and department. View full profiles with papers, SDG contributions, and contact info. Discover collaborators that were invisible before.

**Industry Portal** — Navigate by ESG challenge type: Climate & Carbon, Supply Chain, Governance, Social Impact. Get matched to relevant Gies faculty experts. Access frameworks, policy briefs, and business takeaways derived from academic research.

---

## What Made It More Than a Dashboard

**🤖 AI Chatbot — RAG Architecture** *(my idea)*

I proposed making the platform interactive — not just a place to view data, but a place to have a conversation with it. Built on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the chatbot answers only from verified Gies documents, cites every response, and never guesses.

Design principle: the dashboard shows **WHAT**. The chatbot answers **WHO, WHY, and HOW**.

**🌱 Sustainability Consulting Initiative** *(my idea)*

Seeing the data is step one. Using it is step two. I proposed a student-run ESG consulting model — modeled after Illinois Business Consulting — where industry partners submit a real business challenge and get matched to faculty-advised student teams.

> Without the dashboard → Industry doesn't know Gies has sustainability experts
> With the dashboard → Industry can *see* the expertise
> With the Consulting Initiative → Industry can *use* it

For industry: affordable ESG strategy — supply chain analysis, carbon accounting, disclosure frameworks — without expensive external consultants.
For students: real consulting experience, faculty mentorship, and hands-on sustainability impact.

- Build a RAG-powered chatbot grounded in Gies research — cited, accurate, zero hallucinations

- Deliver a working live prototype within the competition timeline

## User Stories

**As an MBA student**, I want to click on an SDG and immediately see linked papers, authors, and faculty contacts — so I can find my research topic without leaving the platform.

**As a faculty member**, I want to filter colleagues by SDG and department — so I can discover collaborators I didn't know I had.

**As a sustainability director**, I want to navigate by my ESG challenge and get matched to relevant faculty experts — so I can access research-backed strategy without expensive external consultants.
